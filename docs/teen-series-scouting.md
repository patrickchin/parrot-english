# Teen sitcom scene scouting

The goal is about ten playable scenes per season. The list below gives editors
ten places to look in each season of Friends and How I Met Your Mother, with a
Season 1 shortlist for Modern Family and Brooklyn Nine-Nine. Each lead identifies
an episode and an exchange to look for. Exact cuts, speaker turns, suitability,
and reuse permission are pending. This list stays in the authoring notes.

## From lead to playable scene

Use approved source files and record the reuse scope according to the
[scene-content permissions plan](product-plan.md#scene-content-and-permissions).
Audition one continuous exchange
with two or more speaking characters and at least three turns. Keep the setup
and response together. Check suitability for teens, speaker changes and
complete final syllables against the uncut source. A finished entry needs
English dialogue, Chinese hints, timing, role selection, waveform and credits.
Use the [TV review workflow](tv-review.md) and [content pack guide](content-packs.md).
