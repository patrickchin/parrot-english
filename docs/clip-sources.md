# Dubbing scene sources

Prepared on 2026-10-28 for the founder and colleague's adult prototype test.
The player and pack metadata are separate. The four selected meme clips have
**not** been cleared for redistribution. At the owner’s explicit request, the
four prepared excerpts are hosted in dedicated Alibaba OSS object storage
so they load directly. They are not embedded in the ESA function.
Original downloads, prepared meme excerpts, temporary speech transcripts, and the earlier ZIP remain
under the Git-ignored `content/local-dubbing/` directory. Uploading these excerpts
does not establish a redistribution licence.

The 2026-10-29 expansion adds [20 more scenes](extra-clips.md), bringing the
meme shelf to 24. The same OSS storage and unconfirmed-rights status apply.

The first three downloaded sources are fan uploads; availability on YouTube is
not evidence of permission from the original producer. The Shrek reference is
an official upload, which also does not itself grant permission for our edits
or distribution. The actual prepared Shrek footage comes from a
[landscape upload of the same conversation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aJQmVZSAqlc).
Both links remain documented rather than presenting the hosted excerpt as an
officially authorized pack.

`scripts/prepare-meme-packs.mjs` records the exact source offsets, edit choices,
dialogue timings, Chinese hints, and role definitions. It writes metadata to
`public/packs/<scene>/pack.json` and normalized H.264/AAC video to the ignored
authoring directory for a separate upload to OSS.
Existing opaque pack/role/line IDs are retained on reruns.
It requires FFmpeg and the named private source files; it does not fetch files
or credentials during app builds.

Dialogue timing was drafted with local speech recognition and corrected for
the familiar phrases. No speech was generated or cloned. Human timing and
child-suitability review remain required before a child pilot. Wumbo is a
fictional word, so that scene practises performance and pronoun patterns rather
than teaching it as English vocabulary. The excerpt excludes the later
Squidward self-harm joke. The Chocolate excerpt excludes the uploader's
unrelated opening material.

During replay, a recorded take replaces the whole original audio mix for that
character's time window. No separate music/effects stem was supplied for these
four clips, so background sound also drops out during the replaced lines.
Skipped turns retain the original sound. This is a limitation of this prototype,
not source-separated dialogue or an exported finished movie.

- [Publisher's English subtitle file](https://durian.blender.org/wp-content/content/subtitles/sintel_en.srt)

The sample uses source time **02:04.900–02:26.000** from
`Sintel.2010.1080p.mkv`, encoded as a small H.264/AAC MP4. It includes five
dialogue turns. A separate backing excerpt from the publisher's
`sintel-m+e-st.flac` keeps music and effects audible during replaced lines.
The movie, original performances, and backing track were not generated by us.
Three stills were extracted for the poster and character portraits. The player
adds Chinese hints and substitutes locally recorded takes on playback.

This is a quiet conversation excerpt for adult technical testing, not a claim
that the entire fantasy film is appropriate as a young child's learning pack.
The app does not imply endorsement by any film or television producer.

## Verification

- Native MediaRecorder capture, replay, retry, session reset, no-input handling,
  and late permission cancellation were exercised with synthetic microphones
  in desktop and Android-sized Chromium sessions.
- Each of the four prepared excerpts played and accepted a recorded turn.
- WebKit covered playback, layout, missing media, and denied microphone access.
  WebKit microphone capture and physical iPhone/Android devices remain unverified.
- The production build's device file picker was tested using the redistributable
  sample as a fixture; selecting the file made no media upload request.
- Browser-flow tests load the OSS URLs through local media fixtures. Live OSS
  availability, byte-range requests, and CORS were verified after upload; all nine
  uploaded objects matched their authoring files by SHA-256. Four real-OSS scene
  playback checks passed. A real-OSS recording/replay check passed on an isolated
  rerun after an initial concurrent run reset unexpectedly to the role screen.
- Desktop and phone screenshots are stored outside the repository under the
  project's Codex screenshots directory.

## Waveforms and scene thumbnails — 2026-10-29

`node scripts/prepare-waveforms.mjs` derives 5 ms peak envelopes from the actual
prepared clips and extracts small scene thumbnails. Numeric envelopes are
pack metadata; video/audio/JPEG media stays in OSS. Thumbnails use the same source
excerpts and credits above. Learner envelopes are sampled from the microphone
and retained only with the in-memory take. See [UI notes](ui-design.md) for the
shared loudness scale, alignment, source-mix limitation, and recording controls.
