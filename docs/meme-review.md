# Meme scene re-edits — 2026-11-10

Completed the 21 private review re-edits requested after the owner identified
**Patrick**, **Chocolate**, and **Onions** as the right format. Those three
benchmark media files are preserved byte-for-byte. The older-audience folder now
contains **24 active review scenes**: 18 expanded excerpts, three replacement
exchanges, and the three benchmarks.

Each new edit is approximately 10–31 seconds, with 2–4 speaking roles and at
least three dialogue turns. Total new footage is approximately 349 seconds.
The three benchmarks retain their original approximately 11–16 second lengths.
The owner subsequently authorized this batch for the existing internal test
application. The 21 new cuts replace the short excerpts in its older-audience
folder; the three benchmarks remain unchanged.

## Review

Open `content/local-dubbing/review/older-audience/` in the shared checkout.

`index.json` records roles, line/word cues, source ranges, and edit decisions.

The 21 superseded short videos are retained under `superseded-short-cuts/` for
comparison. They are excluded from the active playlist. The original audit is
also kept there. The separate `younger-age-5/` folder still contains the eight
preschool conversations described in [the preschool review](preschool-review.md).

## Reproduce and verify

The shared preparation command serves both actual review cohorts. It does not
write application scenes or upload media:

```sh
node scripts/prepare-review-clips.mjs older-audience
node scripts/prepare-review-clips.mjs older-audience --check
node scripts/prepare-review-clips.mjs younger-age-5 --check
```

`content/meme-review-scenes.json` contains the maintained recipes. Its source
`localFile` paths stay in the ignored authoring directory. Line and word times
are relative to that local source excerpt; `sourceOffset` maps a trimmed source
back to the original upload (170 seconds for *Falling with style*). New cuts
are single continuous ranges. The three benchmark inputs are already prepared
edits, whose original edit notes are retained in their source records.

Bilingual subtitles are relative to the output clip. Cues were aligned against
the actual edited audio using the project's existing torchaudio CTC approach.
Speech recognition on the exported cuts, source frames, and burned-in captions
were cross-checked to correct obvious wording and speaker errors. Adult
listening review remains pending. These are source-authoring tools; learner
recordings and pronunciation scoring are not involved.

Verification covers all 24 active videos: full FFmpeg decoding, audio/video
streams, expected durations, caption completeness and bounds, role/line IDs,
playlist references, and preservation of the three benchmark media digests.
The eight preschool scenes were checked with the shared command after its
rename. Frame sheets are stored under
`~/Pictures/Screenshots/Codex/parrot-english/`.
